## Scope

v0.2.4 prepares the app for the graduation exhibition. It takes the items of
the 2026-10-20 team meeting (as-needed dose recording, a calendar of past
doses, record export, the launcher icon, the UML and the user guide) and the
items left open on the v0.2.3 line. The switches that belong to a public
release (Google Play distribution, App Check enforcement) are out of scope.

## Changes

### As-needed doses

- A dose recorded from the schedule's as-needed card can be undone at once
  from the save notice, as a scheduled dose can; the undo marks only the
  records made by that tap as not recorded. An alert already sent to a
  caregiver is not recalled, but the caregiver's count corrects itself. The
  meeting had leaned to "not needed"; a single wrong tap alerted the
  caregiver and raised the count that the next "N → N+1" confirmation shows,
  and the only correction was three screens deep.
- The history editor offers an as-needed record "taken" or "cancel record
  (recorded by mistake)"; "skipped", which has no meaning without a schedule,
  is shown only for a record already saved that way, and its note no longer
  mentions reminders and missed-dose alerts.
- Several doses of one as-needed medicine on the same day are listed, on
  screen and in the CSV, in the order they were taken; they were ordered by
  their random event id.
- The history's record summary counts scheduled doses only, as the schedule
  screen does; as-needed doses had raised both counts.
- A regimen editor test used a prescription date relative to the real date
  with a fixed clock and failed from 2026-10-20; it is pinned to the clock.
