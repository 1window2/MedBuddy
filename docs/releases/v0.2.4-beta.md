# MedBuddy v0.2.4-beta

## Status

- Version: `0.2.4+25`
- Stable baseline: `v0.2.3-beta`
- Preparation branch: `beta/v0.2.4`
- Target tag: `v0.2.4-beta`
- Platform: Android
- Publication status: published on 2026-10-30 as a limited off-Play GitHub
  pre-release, like v0.2.3, from PR #128; the APK and AAB were built from
  `f377546` (signed run 40842316945), and the commits after it change only

This document records the release boundary. The GitHub pre-release notes
carry the final commit identifiers and signed artifact checksums.

## Scope

v0.2.4 prepares the app for the graduation exhibition. It takes the items of
the 2026-10-20 team meeting (as-needed dose recording, a calendar of past
doses, record export, the launcher icon, the UML and the user guide) and the
items left open on the v0.2.3 line. The switches that belong to a public
release (Google Play distribution, App Check enforcement) are out of scope.

## Changes

### As-needed doses

- A dose recorded from the schedule's as-needed card can be undone at once
  from the save notice, as a scheduled dose can; the undo marks only the
  records made by that tap as not recorded. An alert already sent to a
  caregiver is not recalled, but the caregiver's count corrects itself. The
  meeting had leaned to "not needed"; a single wrong tap alerted the
  caregiver and raised the count that the next "N → N+1" confirmation shows,
  and the only correction was three screens deep.
- The history editor offers an as-needed record "taken" or "cancel record
  (recorded by mistake)"; "skipped", which has no meaning without a schedule,
  is shown only for a record already saved that way, and its note no longer
  mentions reminders and missed-dose alerts.
- Several doses of one as-needed medicine on the same day are listed, on
  screen and in the CSV, in the order they were taken; they were ordered by
  their random event id.
- The history's record summary counts scheduled doses only, as the schedule
  screen does; as-needed doses had raised both counts.
- A phone whose clock ran under a second ahead of the server could not record
  an as-needed dose: the dose time "now" arrived as a future time and was
  refused, and the card said the list had changed. The server now accepts a
  dose time up to 60 seconds ahead (the tolerance it already uses for the
  sign-in time) and stores it as the time it received the request. Found on
  the Galaxy Note10; the emulator follows the computer's clock.
- A regimen editor test used a prescription date relative to the real date
  with a fixed clock and failed from 2026-10-20; it is pinned to the clock.

### Month calendar of past doses

- The medication history, opened from the calendar icon on the schedule
  screen, now starts on the current month. Each day shows how many of its
  scheduled doses were recorded as taken (for example 2/3) with a green,
  amber or grey mark for all, some or none, and a blue ring when an
  as-needed dose was recorded. Tapping a day opens the existing day view;
  its calendar button and the back key return to the month. Earlier months
  stop at the 90-day history window, and later days cannot be tapped. One
  request reads the visible month, so the server is unchanged. Exporting
  from the month starts with that month's dates. Caregivers see the same
  calendar read-only.

### Icons

- The launcher icon is an adaptive icon with the mark only (heart, cross and
  ECG line), inside the safe zone of the 108 dp layer on a white background.
  The Samsung launcher enlarged the old square icon to its squircle and cut
  the "MedBuddy" wordmark at both edges; the name already appears under the
  icon. A monochrome layer serves Android 13 themed icons; Android 7 and
  older keep the old icon.
- The icons are placed by the heart, not by the whole mark's box (2026-10-29,
  chosen with the user from five previews): the V notch between the lobes is
  on the vertical centre line, and the heart sits 1.5 dp (on the launcher
  icon) below its centre of mass, the same share of the mark on every icon.
  The cross above the heart and the ECG tail to its left had made the box
  centre misleading. The launcher mark keeps its size and reaches 25 dp from
  the centre (safe circle 33 dp); the status-bar icon is scaled to keep its
  2 dp margin.
- Reminders and FCM notifications use a transparent status-bar icon with the
  brand colour. Android draws that icon from its transparency, so the opaque
  launcher icon would show as a plain white square.
- The logo source (`frontend/assets/app_icon/medbuddy_logo.png`) has wider
  margins (content at about 0.8 of the old size), drawn at 1080 px from the
  original artwork. The launcher and status-bar icons are generated from it by
  `scripts/generate_android_icons.py`, which now finds the gap above the
  wordmark instead of assuming the old layout, and scales the mark to a fixed
  size, so the icons on the phone look the same as before.

### Reliability, privacy and sharing (2026-10-22)

- A medication history summary (period and counts only) can be shared into a
  linked chat; the card opens the latest records read-only.
- Today's schedule is projected after midnight from an encrypted 14-day
  window kept per account, so doses can be recorded offline; the server
  refuses an offline dose whose regimen changed meanwhile.
- Unconfirmed as-needed doses survive a restart; repeated regular completion
  alerts to caregivers collapse into one; an aborted sign-out restores the
  session's services.
- Server: prescription text is checked for patient information before the
  AI request; retry receipts of deleted medications are emptied (migration
  `e6b4d2a9f031`); rate-limit counters use their own non-evicting Redis;
  retention cleanup is committed with the save.
- Settings show the phone's notification and on-time reminder switches with
  a test notification, in place of the separate setup-check screen.

### Review corrections (2026-10-23)

- The prescription privacy check rejected ordinary prescriptions and bags
  (as-needed and interval directions, "4주 소요", 교부 연월일, pharmacy
  addresses, a patient name on a bag). Identifying lines without medication
  information are now left out and unlabelled dates masked; only a line that
  mixes patient data with a medication row is refused.
- The 12-hour background refresh deleted the offline window; it now fetches
  a new one.
- A shared history card older than 90 days explains that it has expired;
  the card and the share dialog state what the counts mean and what the
  chat receives.
- Resending an earlier day's unconfirmed as-needed dose asks for a new tap
  instead of offering an undo that did nothing.
- The note that one test notification does not guarantee later reminders is
  back.
- Two more regimen editor tests depended on the real date and are pinned.

### Follow-up decisions (2026-10-23)

Recorded in the [decision log](../MedBuddy%20-%20Decision%20Log.md):

- A medication change that fails (for example offline) puts back the
  schedule and offline window it cleared, unless something else was stored
  meanwhile; an applied, conflicting or unknown outcome still clears them.
- The as-needed card shows its list and counts when the device's pending
  store cannot be opened and refuses only a new dose.
- The phone notification switch shows off when the medication channel is
  off.
- A CSV export covers up to 90 days, read in 31-day parts; chat sharing stays
  at 31 days. The CSV drops the internal revision column and its first column
  reads "날짜 (필요시는 기록일)".
- The confirmation for another as-needed dose shows when today's last one
  was taken and how long ago ("마지막 복용 12:30 (2시간 30분 전)"), which
  matters for interval-limited medicines.

### Local feature navigation and presentation

These changes follow the integrated `77a0f26` baseline and describe local source,
not a published build or additional physical-device verification.

- Home adds a feature finder to the left of the notification button. Its 27
  destinations use local Korean/English names, aliases and intent groups, without
  external AI, server search or saved query history. Existing Home cards remain.
- Results reuse existing screens and settings positions. Personal history stays
  owner-scoped; linked-patient lookup refreshes caregiver links and selects a
  patient when needed. Missing/unavailable links lead to connection management.
  Record sharing opens only the existing confirmation dialog after a successful
  initial read. No setting, dose, deletion or transmission runs from search alone.
- The schedule hides partial PRN cards during initial loading while preserving
  their read; later refreshes keep a same-day snapshot. The account welcome panel
  aligns with the full account layout.
- PRN dosage and regimen confirmation use localized dose labels. Android widget
  labels/previews and record-save feedback support English. Notification channel
  names/descriptions follow app language without replacing IDs, scheduled
  notifications or user channel choices.
- Natural-language design and UML add feature-search responsibilities and flow,
  retaining the integrated month history, durable PRN and sharing contracts. See
  [Feature Search](../Feature%20Search.md) and the
  [UML guide](../UML/class/README.md) for scope and validation status.

- The schedule's loading cover is shown only until today's schedule has been
  read once for the account. A patient with only as-needed medicines has an
  empty regular schedule, so every refresh (returning from the history,
  retry, a notification setting change) replaced the usable as-needed card
  with a spinner.
- A "linked patient schedule" search result waits for the link check; if the

  longer pushed over it.
- Search ranking: English concept words that sit inside "notification" (`not`,

- After sign-out the home-screen widget's sign-in prompt follows the app

### Final review corrections (2026-10-26)

- Offline window: a dose upload confirmed while the 14-day window was
  downloading raised the shared cache revision, so the window was refused
  and never fetched again; a user back online with offline doses could be
  left with no window for the next days. A refused window is now fetched
  again when the revision changed.
- A sign-out stopped before the reminders were cancelled (usually the push
  token unregistration failing offline) re-planned the reminders anyway, and
  without an offline window that left only today's. Reminders are now
  re-planned only when their cancellation had started.
- The as-needed undo still works after leaving the schedule; its notice stays
  on screen and used to do nothing there.
- The history opened from a chat card no longer reloads and resets when the
  app redraws (for example on the hourly sign-in token refresh), and Back
  while a day is loading closes the screen instead of being ignored.
- Prescription privacy: a number of two or more digits after a word ending in
  면 ("아프면 12시간", "열나면 10ml") was taken for an address, refusing the
  text or dropping a dose-limit line; "4 주" with a following "소…" medicine
  name was taken for the 주소 label. A label is now checked per line;
  numbers split across lines are still caught.
- A background-refresh test computed the window end from device-local
  midnight and failed east of Korea.
- Left as is: reminders booked by v0.2.3 take the old status-bar icon until
  the updated app or its background work starts once (only Android 8.0 could
  crash on it); undo notices disappear after their time also under TalkBack,
  as all of the app's notices already did.

### Device check corrections (2026-10-26)

- A schedule opened for a slot (a reminder for the evening or bedtime, or
  feature search's "as-needed" result) did not move to that card when it
  started below the screen: the list builds only visible cards, so the card
  did not exist yet. The screen now moves down until the card is built and
  stays at the top for an unknown slot. Found on the Galaxy Note10 with five
  medicines in each slot. On the phone it still waited for a touch: the move
  ran after the schedule refresh in a post-frame callback, and a refresh that
  changed nothing drew no frame. A frame is now requested with it.
- The selected-medication delete button read "삭제 중..." while the
  confirmation was still open; it does so only after "삭제" is pressed.
- Prescription OCR text is sent in on-screen row order. The device recogniser
  returns a pharmacy bag column by column, so the names, the effect labels
  and the "1정씩3회5일분" lines arrived far apart and the AI paired them by
  position; a description fragment read as a medicine name ("코팅정" →
  "코랑정") shifted every later dose by one row. Lines are now ordered by row
  within panels separated by blank vertical gutters, and aligned rows of
  neighbouring columns are kept together; lines are not merged, so the
  device and server privacy checks see the same lines as before. On the
  test bag the AI extraction went from two wrong rows and an extra medicine
  to all five correct in three of three runs.

### Final feature commits (2026-10-29)

Feature-complete state of the line (feature freeze 2026-11-06):

- Medication search modes (fast, balanced, thorough) in Settings, stored on
  the device; the server tries 2, 4 or 8 search keywords, broadens structural
  queries and keeps safe candidates.
- Prescription analysis goes from recognition straight to the medication
  lookup; the review shows lookup summaries at once, result cards collapse,
  and the change radar groups changes into compact categories.
- The cabinet groups saved medicines by date in collapsible cards; home shows
  the MedBuddy wordmark.
- Medication history is shared to other apps as readable text (up to 90 days,
  no file), replacing the CSV file.
- Chat alerts are grouped per conversation with up to three previews and are
  removed when read; messages can be copied; deleted messages and place cards
  read more clearly; a shared hospital or pharmacy reopens with the receiver's
  current details and the operating state at the time it was shared.

### Dependencies (2026-10-25)

- cloudflared 2026.8.2 -> 2026.10.3 (2026-10-27). Cloudflare announced that
  eight eMudhra (emSign) intermediate certificates use weak private keys and
  that cloudflared before 2026.10.2 still trusts them. The tunnel reaches the
  backend over plain HTTP inside the compose network, so no origin
  certificate had to change.

- Pillow 12.3.0 -> 13.0.0. Forty-one Pillow advisories published from
  2026-10-20 to 10-23 (10 critical) are all fixed in 13.0.0; GitHub showed
  82 alerts because `requirements-dev.txt` includes `requirements.txt`. The
  server decodes uploaded pill photos as JPEG, PNG or WebP only, so the TIFF,
  PDF, EPS and GIF issues were not reachable, but the JPEG and PNG ones were.
