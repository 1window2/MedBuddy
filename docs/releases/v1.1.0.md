# MedBuddy 1.1.0

## Status

- Version: `1.1.0+29` (server `1.1.0-rc` until the release)
- Baseline: `v1.0.1`
- Development branch: `v1.1.0`
- Target tag: `v1.1.0`
- Platform: Android
- Publication status: in development

This document records the 1.1.0 boundary. The GitHub release notes carry the final
commit identifiers and signed artifact checksums.

## Changes

- **Release line:** the branches merged before the graduation exhibition were deleted,
  and signed off-Play builds come only from `v1.1.0`.
- **Photo dose recording:** 약 등록 > 사진으로 복용 기록. A photo of the pills in hand is
  matched with the user's own medicines on the server (imprint, or a balanced group of
  look-alike plain tablets; anything weaker is asked), each medicine goes to its own slot
  (late doses flow back to an unhandled slot until the next reminder; a medicine taken in
  the recent-dose spacing (two hours, or half the gap to its previous slot when slots are
  close; four hours for as-needed), or not due yet, is shown and not recorded), and one large
  confirmation records everything in one transaction at the photo time. As-needed
  medicines are recorded once. Design and rules: `docs/Photo Dose Recording.md`.
- **Home:** 약 등록, 약 관리 and 약 검색 are separate green buttons; buttons show only a
  large icon and a name; the four rows share one height without scrolling.
- **Registration:** 낱알약 식별 opens the one-photo screen directly (one or several
  pills); camera/gallery and other choices are large tiles. The per-pill photo mode
  and the screens' unused direct-save paths were removed.
- **Pill identification:** the MFDS words for a logo or score line ("마크", "분할선") are
  no longer compared as imprint text, oval and oblong count as adjacent shapes, an empty
  photo is a photo problem (422) instead of an invalid response, and during a Gemini
  outage the Claude copy's photo verdict is passed on. Found in the 2026-11-28 phone test.
- **Server:** two new endpoints, `POST /schedule/photo-intake/plan` and
  `/schedule/photo-intake/confirm`; version `1.1.0-rc`. No migration or new
  configuration. Deploy the backend before distributing the app (older servers
  answer 404 and the app shows a connection problem).
- **Documentation:** UML (UC-39, `PhotoDoseRecording` sequence, class diagrams with the
  Claude outage fallback) updated; the user guide PDFs are not yet updated.
